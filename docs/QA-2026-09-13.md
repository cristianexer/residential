# Explorer corrections — 13 September 2026

## Reproduced failures

- Two simultaneous tracks: native range plus decorative blue–gold progress.
- A 31.8 × 15.3 metre opaque section rectangle covered all room voids.
- Small disassembly offsets, compounded child displacements, and camera
  refitting on every slider input obscured the exploded structure.
- Pointer-down selection only chose buildings/homes; clipped geometry could
  intercept the click.
- Full-face graphite and trim overlays covered openings. Ground pavement and
  duplicated floor finishes occupied overlapping surfaces.
- Cars rotated individual parts independently and did not share bay coordinates.
- Blender's MCP connection uses BLENDER_MCP_PORT, not BLENDER_PORT.
- Blender 5.2 partial scene writing crashed in BKE_view_layer_copy_data when a
  newly populated export scene's view layer had not been evaluated.

## Implemented and checked

- Exactly one keyboard-operable blue–gold range control.
- Immutable assembly transforms; floors, roof, ceilings and façade assemblies
  separate independently without cumulative drift. Dragging does not reframe.
- Per-solid triangle/plane section caps. Windowed walls carry original solid
  cell volumes so caps do not invent filled window openings.
- Visible-component picking, including cap-to-source selection; frame, isolate,
  restore and a textual component catalogue.
- Hollow window trim, real voids, graphite side bands, non-overlapping floor
  levels, and stair flights through slab openings.
- 68 illustrative bays and 20 cars, checked in the shared bay coordinate frame.
  All vehicle parts fit inside their bay; tire bottoms meet pavement; markings
  are above pavement and no bay intersects a building footprint.
- 192 windows checked against actual façade faces for blocking geometry.
- Leaf geometry, tree branches, normal maps, per-material texture scales,
  calibrated day lighting, blue sky and versioned GLB loading.
- Export only project-owned objects. Preservation checks include foreign user
  objects/collections linked within the generated hierarchy.
- Draw batching preserves source IDs, transforms, visibility and picking;
  isolated material state prevents clipping leaking between buildings/site.

## Boundaries

This pass fixes the reported controls and geometry defects; it is not a claim
that the entire original photorealism plan is finished. Furniture and vehicles
remain illustrative custom assets; fully verified room dimensions, baked
interior GI, photo-scanned surfaces, production collision/navigation coverage,
three-browser certification, mobile performance, and a Vite/TypeScript migration
are not established by these checks. The 30.30/6.60 m² figures are supplied
facts; the checks do not certify an architectural net-area calculation.

Hullscope was inspected for selection, visibility, explosion and camera
patterns. The saved Lloyds checkout path no longer exists locally, so its source
was not inspected. No sibling project was modified.
